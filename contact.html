Support

For support with any of our games, including Mahjong Duel and Polygon Paradise, email us at studiostnd@gmail.com.

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • The name of the game
  • Your device model and OS version
  • A short description of the problem (screenshots help!)

We aim to respond within a few business days.

Privacy and data requests

To request deletion of data tied to your account, or for any questions about how we handle your information, email studiostnd@gmail.com or see our Privacy Policy.
